# Code Of Conduct

[English](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) | [Русский](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.ru.md)

MeetingAgent is an early open-source project. Contributors and users are expected to keep discussions practical, respectful, and focused on improving the software.

## Expected Behavior

- Be clear and constructive.
- Discuss technical tradeoffs with evidence.
- Respect privacy and do not share private project data.
- Help keep issues and pull requests reproducible.
- Disclose security-sensitive concerns responsibly.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private transcripts, customer documents, credentials, or tokens.
- Using issues or pull requests to expose sensitive data.
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive behavior.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that violates this policy and may restrict participation when needed to protect contributors, users, or private data.
